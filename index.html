CNT 4714 Project 2 Concurrency Architecture

Synchronized Multithreaded Banking & Regulatory Simulator

Multi-Agent Thread Topology & Lock Protocol

[1. Agent Workpool Architecture (19 Simultaneous Threads)]
• 5 Depositor Agents (DT0–DT4): Generate random credits ($1–$600) into randomly selected accounts. Never block; signal condition variables on every completed deposit.
• 10 Withdrawal Agents (WT0–WT9): Request debits ($1–$99). Block on insufficient funds using Condition await() until signaled.
• 2 Transfer Agents (TR0–TR1): Execute atomic inter-account balance migrations holding locks on both accounts simultaneously.
• 1 Internal Auditor & 1 U.S. Treasury Auditor: Perform atomic system-wide audits verifying combined liquidity across both accounts simultaneously.
[2. Synchronization & Lock Specifications]
• Explicit ReentrantLock Protocol: Zero use of Java monitors or the synchronized keyword. Employs java.util.concurrent.locks.ReentrantLock with condition variables.
• Executor Framework: Controlled via Executors.newFixedThreadPool(19) with random sleep-decay balancing operational frequency.
[3. Federal Regulatory Compliance Engine]
• Money Laundering Suppression Act (1994): Automatic Currency Transaction Report (CTR) flagging for all deposits > $450.00 and withdrawals > $90.00.
• Real-Time Persistence: Atomic writing of timestamped, numbered audit records into transactions.tsv.
